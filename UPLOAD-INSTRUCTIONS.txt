WHAT CHANGED

TOP-RIGHT ACCOUNT PHOTO
- When a citizen has a profile photo, that photo now fills the top-right account circle.
- Citizens without a profile photo still see their account initial.
- The existing click behavior for opening the citizen's own profile remains intact.

18+ ACCOUNT REQUIREMENT
- New account creation now requires date of birth.
- The browser checks for age 18+ before signup.
- Supabase independently enforces the 18+ requirement server-side, so changing browser code cannot bypass it.
- Date of birth is stored privately and is not added to the public citizen profile.

USER AGREEMENT / TERMS
- New users must open the User Agreement & Terms of Use.
- The acceptance checkbox remains disabled until the user reaches the agreement confirmation and clicks "I have read this agreement."
- They must then check the agreement box before creating an account.
- Supabase independently requires the accepted Terms version during signup.
- Current Terms version: 2026-10-23-v1.
- A User Agreement tile is also available from More after login.

IMPORTANT LEGAL NOTE
These Terms provide useful account rules, disclosures, consent records, and an 18+ requirement.
They do NOT guarantee protection from all legal claims or satisfy every law that may apply.
Have a qualified attorney review the Terms, Privacy Policy, payment/refund rules, and business
structure before treating them as production legal documents.

FOUNDER EXECUTIVE ACTIONS
- Government now contains an official Executive Actions archive.
- Only the real Founder & Head of Nation account can sign:
  * Executive Orders
  * Proclamations
- This restriction is enforced in Supabase using the existing private Founder identity mapping.
- Ordinary citizens cannot bypass the restriction by editing browser code.
- Executive Orders receive EO-001, EO-002, etc.
- Proclamations receive P-001, P-002, etc.
- Signed text is immutable.
- The Founder can revoke an action, but its signed record remains in the archive.
- Executive actions are clearly described as internal Staralia governance records, not real-world governmental authority.
