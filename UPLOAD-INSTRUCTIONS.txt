CORE GRAMMAR DISPLAYED
- Subject → Verb → Object
- da = past
- sha = future
- ne = negation
- ke = question marker
- -en / -in = regular plural
- adjectives and possessives follow nouns
- no grammatical articles equivalent to a/an/the
- gender-neutral pronoun system
