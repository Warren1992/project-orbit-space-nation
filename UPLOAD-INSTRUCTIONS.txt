STARALIAN CALENDAR
Official epoch:
0001 Aurelis 01 = January 1, 2026

Official date order:
YEAR · MONTH · DAY
Example:
0001 Nythera 27

12 MONTHS

Each month contains exactly 30 days.

THE PASSAGE
After Zenithar 30:
Passage 01 — Founding Day
Passage 02 — Citizenship Day
Passage 03 — Day of Consciousness
Passage 04 — Exploration Day
Passage 05 — Dawn Day

Earth leap years add:
Passage 06 — Continuance Day

This keeps January 1 aligned with Aurelis 01 every year.

CALENDAR PAGE
- Added a Calendar tile to More / Nation navigation.
- Automatically displays today's current Staralian date.
- Shows the Earth date beneath it.
- Shows all 12 months and their meanings.
- Shows all Passage holidays.
- Includes a two-way converter:
  * Earth → Staralia
  * Staralia → Earth
- Dates before January 1, 2026 are intentionally outside the Staralian era.

NEWS
Every official news story now displays two dates on the same story card:
LEFT:
Staralia · 0001 Nythera 27

RIGHT:
Earth (U.S.) · October 24, 2026

Both dates are derived automatically from the story's existing published_at timestamp.
No extra date entry is required when the President publishes a story.

VALIDATED EXAMPLES

Round-trip Earth ↔ Staralia conversion was tested across every day of 2026, 2027, and 2028.
