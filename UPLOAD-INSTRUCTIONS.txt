GOVERNMENT STRUCTURE

LEGISLATIVE BRANCH
- Congress consists of ONE chamber only: the Senate.
- The Senate has exactly 100 seats.
- The Founder & Head of Nation is part of the Executive Branch and is no longer a voting Senator.
- Senators are seated through Senate elections.
- Active Senators introduce bills, debate, comment, and cast roll-call votes.
- The Executive can no longer directly close Senate bill voting.
- Senate voting is finalized automatically after the voting deadline.

EXECUTIVE BRANCH
- Citizen #000001, Founder & Head of Nation, serves as Staralia's chief executive.
- A Senate-passed bill is presented to the Executive.
- The Founder can:
  * Sign the bill into Staralia law
  * Veto the bill
  * Issue Executive Orders
  * Issue Proclamations
  * Nominate Supreme Court justices
- Signed laws receive sequential Staralia Law numbers (S.L. 001, S.L. 002, etc.).
- A veto opens a 7-day Senate override vote.
- A two-thirds Senate override turns the vetoed bill into law.
- The Founder cannot directly appoint or remove Senators under this model.

JUDICIAL BRANCH
- Staralia now has a 9-seat Supreme Court:
  * Seat 1: Chief Justice
  * Seats 2–9: Associate Justices
- The Founder nominates justices.
- The Senate votes to confirm or reject nominations.
- Citizens may file constitutional-review cases.
- Active justices can vote to uphold, strike, or dismiss a challenged Staralia government action.
- A majority/quorum of active justices is required before a final Court decision can be issued.

CHECKS AND BALANCES
1. Senator introduces legislation.
2. Senate votes.
3. Passed bill goes to the Executive.
4. Executive signs or vetoes.
5. Senate may override a veto with two-thirds support.
6. Supreme Court can review constitutional challenges within Staralia.
