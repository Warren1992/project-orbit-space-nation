WHY VENUS?

◉

EARTH-LIKE GRAVITY

At Venus, gravity is about 90% of Earth’s — close enough for long-term human comfort.

☀

ABUNDANT SOLAR ENERGY

Closer to the Sun, Venus receives far more sunlight than Earth — powering the future.

⌂

BREATHABLE AIR INSIDE

At cloud level, ordinary breathable air could also act as a lifting gas.
